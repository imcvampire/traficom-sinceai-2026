## Assumptions

- `A-T-ANT-MAX`: Several antenna rows: the one with the highest e.r.p. is assessed.
- `A-T-ANT-OMNI`: No antenna diagram in the notice: the antenna is treated as non-directional at the notified maximum power.
- `A-T-BILATERAL`: Bilateral agreements (FIN–RUS, FIN–EST, Nordic) override the indicative criteria; their contents are not in the repository.
- `A-T-BORDER-SAMPLE`: The Finnish border and coastline are sampled every 5 km (Natural Earth 1:50m); the 25 nearest points are evaluated.
- `A-T-BW-CHANNEL`: The occupied bandwidth (bdwdth) stands in for the channel spacing or block size in the criterion's bandwidth correction.
- `A-T-ECC-NONPREF`: PCI and synchronisation-frequency alignment are not in the notice: the most restrictive ECC/REC/(11)04 border level (41 dB(µV/m)/5 MHz) is assessed.
- `A-T-ECC-RUS`: Russia's CEPT membership is suspended: ECC criteria applied to RUS notices are indicative, and the FIN–RUS bilateral agreement governs.
- `A-T-FLAT`: Flat terrain and no clutter: P.1546 without terrain clearance angle or terminal heights (tca_not_applied). Conservative for screening; overstates reach over hills and into towns.
- `A-T-H1-AGL`: h1 is the antenna height above ground (hgt_agl), not the effective height of Annex 5 section 3, because the effective height needs terrain data.
- `A-T-LAND-MASK`: Land and sea along the path come from Natural Earth 1:10m land rasterised at 0.01°; short coastal segments can be misclassified. Lakes count as land, and so does everything outside the clip box (0-45°E, 50-75°N), including the Barents Sea east of 45°E.
- `A-T-PUB-PARTS`: Publication part meanings (1 received, 2 recorded with findings, 3 returned) are inferred from the data and to be confirmed against the BR IFIC Preface.
- `A-T-SEA-COLD`: Sea segments are cold sea (Baltic, Gulf of Finland, Barents Sea).
- `A-T-TIER1-3DB`: Tier 1 screens out a notice whose upper bound at the nearest Finnish point (the larger of the all-sea and all-land values; below about 100 MHz land can exceed cold sea) is at least 3 dB below the trigger.
